Manufacturing Pipeline

The Four Pillars of Our Shoemaking Process

From initial digital conceptualization to final box dispatch, every footwear silhouette follows our stringent quality-controlled pipeline.

  1. 01

    3D Lasting & CAD Prototyping

    Digital shoe lasts are sculpted and analyzed for ergonomic pressure distribution prior to CNC cutting, ensuring an exact anatomical fit.

  2. 02

    Ethical Material Curation

    Leathers, recycled ocean plastics, and organic cottons are batch-tested for tensile strength, abrasion durability, and environmental compliance.

  3. 03

    Laser Skiving & Stitching

    Precision skived edges and heavy-gauge industrial stitching assemble upper patterns with reinforced stress points for maximum longevity.

  4. 04

    Sole Molding & Inspection

    Direct-injected EVA or Goodyear welted leather soles are cured under heat and pressure, followed by a 10-point manual inspection.
